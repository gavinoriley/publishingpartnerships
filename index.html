In this platformer, you control a piece of tofu named Hamachi, who unexpectedly falls into the possession of Demon Queen Deity, an evil fiend who intends to use him as a vessel to conquer the world through mass murder.

FEATURES:

  • Play as the villain: Choose to kill, spare, or ignore every civilian.
  • Three distinct routes: Pacifist, Neutral, and Genocide, each with its own ending.
  • Defeat or pacify demonic bosses, pacified bosses will join your side.
  • Journey through 8 visually distinct worlds built with original pixel art.
  • Chainsaw power-up, The Psycho Saw, grants temporary invincibility and destroys any being you touch.
  • Collect souls from dead civilians or sushi dropped by Penelope Pom Pom Pink to restore health.
  • Your crimes will not go unnoticed by the authorities.

ABOUT THE DEV

Based in California, my name is gavinoriley. I'm a solo self-taught developer, or, more specifically, I barf up code into GameMaker and then strategically piece it together with glue and tape.

I began by pulling apart prebuilt projects in GameMaker to understand how code and design fit together, while also modding existing 2D and 3D games. That eventually led me to a specialized game development program in high school, where I continued honing my skills.

Today, I'm the independent dev behind Hamachi the Psychotic Killer, which I produced from concept through release, collaborating with an artist on the game's tilesets and a composer on its music.

The platformer game where you can play as the BAD GUY. (or the good guy)

Venture through a world where your actions have consequences.
In this platformer, you control a piece of tofu named Hamachi, who unexpectedly falls into the possession of Demon Queen Deity,
an evil fiend who intends to use him as a vessel to conquer the world through mass murder.

Spare or kill innocent civilians with your axe, or with a chainsaw that grants invincibility!

Fight against powerful demons, or show them you mean no harm and pacify them!

Your crimes will not go unnoticed by the authorities.
